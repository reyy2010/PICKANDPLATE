A SMARTER KITCHEN

Little tips, big difference.

Make your ingredients last longer and make everyday cooking feel easier.

01

Store food properly

Keep herbs wrapped in a lightly damp towel and store leftovers in sealed containers.

02

Use leftovers creatively

Turn yesterday's vegetables into an omelette, soup, stir-fry or pasta sauce.

03

Plan before shopping

Check your fridge before buying groceries and make a short list of what you really need.

04

Keep a visible list

Write down ingredients that should be used soon and plan a meal around them.
