About Us

What's in My Fridge?

A smart and simple website that helps you decide what to cook using the ingredients you already have at home.
Instead of buying more food or letting ingredients go to waste, you can enter the ingredients available in your fridge,
and the website will suggest suitable recipes you can make with them.

How to Use..?

1. Add your ingredients:
->Enter the ingredients you currently have.

2. Find your recipe:
->Click Find Recipe to get suitable meal suggestions.

3. Choose a recipe:
->Explore the suggested recipes and choose what you like.

4. Start cooking..!:
->Follow the ingredients and steps to prepare your meal.
